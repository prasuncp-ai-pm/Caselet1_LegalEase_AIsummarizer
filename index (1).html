Caselet: Business Value Modeling for
Generative AI Feature

You are a Product Manager at LegalEase, a SaaS platform that helps businesses store and manage their legal documents. Your company is considering building a new Generative AI feature called “QuickSummarizer” that helps users summarize long legal documents and answer questions about their content.

Objective

Evaluate the business value of building QuickSummarizer and recommend whether to proceed, pivot, or stop.

Your Role

  • Product Manager at LegalEase
  • Own problem definition, user value, business value and recommendation
  • Work cross-functionally with Engineering, Design, Data and Finance

Key Actions

  • Define the user problem and opportunity
  • Model the business value and costs
  • Identify risks and assumptions
  • Recommend a decision with rationale

Primary Objective

Determine if QuickSummarizer creates enough business value to justify investment and drive measurable impact on growth, retention and efficiency.

Caselet Summary

LegalEase users often store long and complex legal documents (e.g., NDAs, MSAs, contracts). Users struggle to quickly understand key terms, obligations and risks. Support teams also spend significant time answering repetitive document-related questions.

Key Context

  • Product: LegalEase SaaS platform for legal document management
  • Proposed Feature: QuickSummarizer — Generative AI feature
  • Users: Legal, Procurement, Sales and Business Teams
  • Business Goals: Improve retention, reduce support costs, increase product stickiness

Success Hypothesis

If we help users summarize and ask questions about their legal documents instantly, they will find more value in LegalEase, use it more frequently, rely less on support, and be less likely to churn.

Key Assumptions

  • Current annual recurring revenue (ARR): ~₹30 Cr
  • Current churn rate: ~10% annually
  • Support cost related to legal queries: ~₹3.5 Cr annually
  • AI feature drives measurable engagement uplift
  • Adoption reaches critical mass within 6–9 months
  • Model excludes implementation and ongoing AI costs
PM watch-out

These are caselet assumptions. In a real investment memo, validate every input before using it as a forecast.

Key Learnings

Start with the business problem

Do not justify GenAI because the technology exists. Link the capability to a customer problem and measurable business outcome.

Make value explicit

Retention, support savings, adoption and engagement create a much stronger investment narrative than feature usage alone.

Guardrails are part of the product

For legal AI, groundedness, uncertainty, source traceability, privacy and escalation to counsel are first-class requirements.
