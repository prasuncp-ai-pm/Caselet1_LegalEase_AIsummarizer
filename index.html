The Caselet

Company context, customer problem and the product spark.

Company

LegalEase Solutions was founded in 2023 and is a rapidly growing Bengaluru SaaS startup targeting Indian SMEs.

  • Repository of pre-vetted templates and clause libraries.
  • Helps businesses organize, manage and secure sensitive business/legal documents.
  • Core workflows: store, track and collaborate on contracts, compliance documents and EULAs.

Customer Problem

Technical strength is not translating into retention: churn is rising and acquisition is getting harder.

  • SMEs often lack in-house legal teams.
  • Legal jargon is difficult to interpret.
  • Customers pay local lawyers to translate basic legal language.
  • They like document organization but still leave.

Product Spark

QuickSummarizer: an AI-powered summarizer for dense legal documents.

  • Plain-language summary.
  • Key obligations, risks and benefits.
  • Future potential: document Q&A and clause explanations.
  • Designed to empower users, not replace lawyers.

Customer-Facing Press Release

Customer promises and product USP.

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

LegalEase Launches QuickSummarizer to Help Indian SMEs Understand Complex Legal Documents in Minutes

LegalEase today announced QuickSummarizer, an AI-powered document-understanding capability that turns dense legal documents into concise, plain-language explanations of key obligations, risks and important clauses.

It helps founders, procurement, sales, operations and legal teams understand what matters faster, while keeping qualified legal counsel in the loop for decisions requiring expert judgment.

Customer Promises + Product USP

  • Clarity: understand the essence of a document quickly.
  • Speed: reduce time spent searching and interpreting.
  • Confidence: surface obligations, risks and terms.
  • Traceability: connect important outputs to source content.
  • Safety: assist understanding; do not replace legal counsel.
USP: LegalEase combines document context, legal workflow integration, enterprise controls and purpose-built UX — rather than generic AI chat disconnected from the document system.

AI Summary

This Master Services Agreement governs professional services between Company A and Company B. It establishes confidentiality, service scope, payment and termination expectations. Services are controlled through Statements of Work and acceptance criteria.

Term: two years from the Effective Date.
Services: governed by Statements of Work.
Payment: invoices payable within 30 days.
Confidentiality: both parties protect shared confidential information.
Termination: written notice under the agreement.
Ask the document

Try: “What are the payment terms?”, “What are our obligations?” or “When can this agreement be terminated?”

Effective Date: date on which the agreement is fully executed.
Confidential Information: non-public information disclosed in connection with the agreement.
Statement of Work: defines service scope and delivery details.
Payment: Net 30 from invoice receipt.
Payment exposure: verify that 30-day terms and remedies fit the commercial arrangement.
SOW dependency: service obligations depend on each Statement of Work.
Legal review: AI explains content; it does not determine enforceability or provide legal advice.

Recommended
PROCEED WITH MVP

The strongest case is not “GenAI is cool.” It is that QuickSummarizer addresses a documented customer pain tied to churn, with quantified retention and support economics.

  • 5% churn reduction → ₹1.5 Cr retained ARR over 18 months.
  • 15% fewer legal-related tickets → ₹50 L potential annual savings.
  • Future premium AI tier provides upside.
  • Controlled rollout can prove incremental impact.
Conditions

Not a blank cheque.

  • Validate baseline churn and ticket economics.
  • Quantify build, model/API and operating costs.
  • Set AI quality and legal-safety gates.
  • Measure against a control where feasible.
  • Scale only after customer and unit economics are proven.
